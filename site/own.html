    Using it for real

    This page is a trial: it tells you whether AI routing would be safe on your messages. Running it on every new message is a bigger job. Here is what changes, and why.

    1. Connect the helpdesk. Zendesk, Freshdesk or a shared inbox sends each new message to a small server, which sets the team and leaves an internal note with the reason. Nobody copies CSVs around.
    2. Your own AI account, paid. This demo uses Gemini's free tier, which Google may use to improve its products. Real customer messages need a paid account (Google Cloud's Montreal region keeps data in Canada) and a check against PIPEDA and your own policies.
    3. Shadow mode first. For two to four weeks it only suggests, as a note, and people route as usual. Comparing its suggestions with what people did is the real test. Automatic routing waits until those numbers hold up.
    4. Learn from corrections. When someone moves a message to another team, that's recorded and the plain model is retrained weekly on the latest history.
    5. Watch for drift. A new product or outage changes what customers write about. A weekly accuracy check, with an alert when it drops, catches that before customers do.
    6. Keep a record. Every decision, both opinions and the reason, kept for review, so any routing can be explained after the fact.

    What to keep in mind about this trial

    • Your file is read and the plain model is trained in your browser. Only the sample of up to 200 messages goes to the AI, through a small server of mine, with emails, links, postcodes and long numbers hidden first. Nothing is stored.
    • The AI part uses Gemini's free tier, so don't upload messages you aren't allowed to share with Google.
    • A few hundred labelled messages give a rough answer. The fewer examples a team has, the weaker the plain model is for that team, and the more messages will be held for a person.
