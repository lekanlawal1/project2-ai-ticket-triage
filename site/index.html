AI ticket triage · bank support

Every message, to the right gate.

An AI reads each customer message and picks the team that should handle it. A plain model trained on 10,000 examples gives a second opinion. A message only goes straight to a team when both agree and the AI can quote the words that decided it. Everything else goes to a person.

    04How it works

    1. The AI reads it. Gemini picks one of seven teams and a priority, says how sure it is, and quotes the words that decided it. Its answer has to fit a fixed shape, so it can't invent a team.
    2. A plain model reads it too. Word counts and logistic regression, trained on Banking77's 10,003 training messages. It runs in your browser and never sees the AI's answer.
    3. Code decides. Straight to a team only if both pick the same one, the AI is at least 80% sure, and its quote is really in the message. Otherwise a person checks it, with the reasons shown.

    What to keep in mind

    • Banking77's messages are short, written for one bank, and in English. Real inboxes are messier.
    • The seven teams are my grouping of Banking77's 77 request types, written before any scoring. Some requests honestly fit two teams.
    • The AI's "how sure" is its own estimate. The results above check it against what actually happened.
    • Messages you type go to Google's Gemini API through a small server of mine. They aren't stored, but don't type anything private.
